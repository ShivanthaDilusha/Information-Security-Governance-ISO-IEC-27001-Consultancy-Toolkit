# Information-Security-Governance-ISO-IEC-27001-Consultancy-Toolkit
Developed an end-to-end Information Security Management System (ISMS) implementation toolkit and strategic roadmap based on the updated ISO/IEC 27001:2022 standard for CyberLabs. Designed from the perspective of an Information Security Consultant, this project bridges C-suite business justification with hands-on technical risk governance.

Key Achievements & Deliverables:

Executive Business Case & Financial Modeling: Formulated a $45,000 implementation business case featuring cost-benefit analysis, payback period estimation (<18 months), and ROI projections (324%) to align security investments with strategic business goals.

Risk Assessment & Treatment Architecture: Built custom Asset Registries and CIA (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability) Risk Matrices to evaluate threats, calculate inherent vs. residual risk, and define actionable Risk Treatment Plans across IT environments.

Core Security Policy Framework: Authored key organizational security policies and operational workflows, including Access Control Lifecycle, Incident Response Workflows, Password & Authentication Policies, and Physical Security Protocols.

Compliance Checklist & Annex A Controls: Mapped organizational controls against ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clauses 4–10 and selected Annex A controls (e.g., Threat Intelligence A.5.7, Access Rights A.8.9, Web App & Secure Coding A.8.23/28).

Continual Improvement & Audit Preparedness: Integrated Non-Conformity and Corrective/Preventive Action Report (NCPAR) templates alongside the Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA) framework to ensure audit readiness and ongoing ISMS maturity.
